⚙️ How It Works

Four stages, from pixels to alarm — pure computer vision, no training data, runs on CPU.

1

Colour Segmentation

Each frame goes to HSV colour space; red→orange→yellow pixels that are bright and saturated are extracted as flame candidates.

2

Noise Cleanup

Morphological open/close removes speckles. Only blobs above a minimum area count — tiny glints are ignored.

3

Flicker Check

Real flames dance. The flame area must jitter frame-to-frame — this rejects static orange objects like lamps or posters.

4

Temporal Alarm

Fire must be confirmed in 5 of the last 10 frames before the alarm fires; 15 quiet frames reset it. No single-frame false alarms.
